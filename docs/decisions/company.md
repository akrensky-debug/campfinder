# Company rules

*How this company is set up and run. Decided 28 September 2026. These come from experience,
and they hold unless Andrew changes them in writing here.*

## Ownership

1. **One founder.** Andrew owns the company. There is no co-founder until someone has earned
   it, and earning it means paper: a grant from the pool, on the standard form, with the
   standard schedule. Titles do not create ownership. Friendship does not create ownership.
2. **One form, one schedule, for everyone.** Every equity grant uses the same restricted
   stock or option form with the same vesting: four years, one-year cliff. If anyone's terms
   differ, the difference is written in the board consent in plain words, and Andrew reads
   that table before he signs.
3. **Nothing counts until it is executed.** A draft, a handshake, a call, "you can take that
   to the bank": none of it is the deal. The deal is the signed document, and the signed
   document gets read in full, on a screen big enough to read it, before signing. Never
   sign a signature packet without the documents it belongs to.
4. **Andrew's own lawyer reviews anything that touches equity, control or his own role**,
   before signing, every time. Company counsel works for the company.

## Control

5. **Andrew is the board.** The board does not expand as a favour, a gesture, or a step in
   someone else's vesting. If it ever expands, Andrew gets something contractual in return
   the same day: a voting agreement, protective provisions, and his own employment
   agreement with defined cause and indemnification.
6. **Know the definitions in the documents.** What "termination of service" means, how many
   days the forfeiture clock runs, who can remove an officer and who can remove a director.
   Written down here when the documents exist.
7. **Raise structural problems before the ink, never after.** Leverage exists before
   signing. After signing it exists only in what was signed.

## Paper for everyone's time

8. **No work without paper.** Anyone who gives the company time has a document: employee,
   contractor or advisor, with a scope, a rate or a grant, and an end. Deferred salary is
   a written liability, not a favour.
9. **IP is assigned to the company on day one**, by Andrew and by everyone after him. The
   company owns the code, the brand and the data. The GitHub organisation, the domain, the
   app store accounts and the vendor accounts belong to the company, with Andrew as owner.

## Records

10. **Andrew keeps his own copies of everything he signs**, on his own account and his own
    machine, the day he signs. Executed documents, the signing record, the cap table.
11. **Legal and equity correspondence goes through personal email**, never only through a
    company address that can be switched off.
12. **The 83(b) election is filed within 30 days of any grant**, with proof of mailing kept.
13. **A dated record of decisions.** Board consents, this file, and the git history. The
    same rule the product applies to listings applies to the company: every change has a
    date and an author.

## Money

14. **The company does not depend on any one person's day job, contacts or capital** to
    exist. Counsel, the bank and the registered agent are chosen by the company and known
    to Andrew directly.

## What this is not

This is not a plan for a fight and it does not assume bad faith from anyone who works with
us. It is the paperwork that lets a partnership stay a friendship.
