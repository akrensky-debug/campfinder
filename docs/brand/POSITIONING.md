# Positioning

*One page. Draft for Andrew to edit. "CampFinder" is a placeholder until the name is decided.*

## In one line

Unlock the collective intelligence of parents to make summer camps easier.

## What we are

The place New England parents plan and book the whole summer, and the place camps get found and booked without learning new software.

## Who it's for

**Parents** with kids aged 5 to 14 who have to cover ten weeks, often for more than one child, around work. They plan in January and February. They ask other parents first and Google second.

**Camp owners** who run a town rec program, a farm camp, a sailing club, a church day camp, or a sports week. Their tools are a PDF brochure, a Google Form, checks or Venmo, and a spreadsheet. They fill spots by word of mouth and hate paperwork.

## What we're not

Not a directory. Directories go stale the day they launch.
Not an ad network. Nobody buys their way to the top of a parent's list.
Not camp software. Owners never log in unless they want to.

## Why us

**The data stays current.** Owners change their listing by replying to an email or a text. Every change is dated, and parents see the date.

**Parents who've been there.** After each camp week, two taps: did your kid love it, would you go back, one thing other parents should know. Shared only in aggregate. Nobody else has this.

**One profile, every camp.** Fill in your kid's forms once. We send them to each camp you book.

**The trust rules, published.** Parents own their data, can export it and delete it. We never sell it, never show ads, never train shared models on children's data. Recommendations are never for sale.

## How we sound

Like a parent who has done this before. Plain and direct. Specific numbers over adjectives. We say when we don't know something. No exclamation points, no "seamless," no jargon a camp director would have to look up.

## Proof we can point to

- A date on every fact, and the owner who confirmed it.
- Parent feedback shown on listings, in aggregate, from the first summer.
- The trust rules, in full, on a page anyone can read.

## The two messages

**To parents:** Plan the whole summer in one place, guided by parents who've been there.

**To camp owners:** Get found and booked. Nothing to learn. Reply to one email.
